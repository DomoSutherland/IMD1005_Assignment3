~ meet the maker ~

Hi, I'm Mia — dreamer & maker

I grew up filling notebooks with sketches of forests, pressed flowers, and half-finished love letters to fictional characters from the anime I'd watch until 2am. Journaling was never a habit for me — it was a sanctuary.

✦   ✦   ✦

How Moonlit Pages came to be

During a particularly grey winter, I started hand-binding journals just for myself, filling them with watercolour illustrations and little pocket envelopes for secrets and train tickets. Friends noticed. They wanted their own. So I made more.

In 2022, I opened a tiny Etsy shop. Within three months, my bedroom had become a studio — walls covered in paper swatches, shelves lined with washi tape and ink pots, fairy lights strung up for atmosphere.

✦   ✦   ✦

What inspires everything I make

Studio Ghibli. The smell of rain. Fairy tales. That specific golden hour feeling where everything looks slightly magical. The way certain books feel like stepping into a different world entirely.

I design for people who collect beauty on purpose — for the girls who have a corner of their room that is entirely their own, filled with things that make their soul feel full.

Our Studio Values

✦
  • ✋

    Handmade with Heart

    Every single piece is crafted by hand, one at a time. No mass production. No shortcuts. Just time, care, and intention.

  • 🌿

    Thoughtfully Made

    We use eco-conscious materials where we can — recycled papers, soy-based inks, and plastic-free packaging that feels as beautiful as what's inside.

    We believe creativity is medicine. Our products are designed to make you feel something — and to give you a space to feel it safely.
